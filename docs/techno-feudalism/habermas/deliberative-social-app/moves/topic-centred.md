# Topic-Centred, Not Personality-Centred

> See [moves README](README.md) for orientation. This is the first design move.

The unit of organisation is the *matter of common concern*, not the user. Posts attach to topics, threads, or questions; user profiles are minimal and not the primary navigation.

- *Why:* Representational publicness centres the lord; deliberative publicness centres the issue. (See [refeudalization-1962](../../refeudalization-1962.md) for the original concept.)
- *Cost:* No virality through personality. Slower growth. Public figures find it less rewarding.
