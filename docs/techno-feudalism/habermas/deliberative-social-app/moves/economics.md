# Reader-Funded Economics

> See [moves README](README.md) for orientation. This is the sixth design move.

Revenue from participants, not from advertisers or data buyers. Multiple models can coexist:

- Subscription per community.
- Voluntary contribution.
- Micro-payment for premium features.
- Cooperative ownership where the platform is owned by its users.

- *Why:* The bourgeois public sphere was funded congruently with its function ([bourgeois-public-sphere](../../bourgeois-public-sphere.md)); a deliberative platform must be too.
- *Cost:* Subscriptions exclude the unwilling-to-pay. Cooperative governance is slow and contested. Free-platform competitors will always have more reach.

The economic question is the hardest unsolved problem in the sketch — see [tensions](../tensions.md).
