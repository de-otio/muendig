# Identity Sovereignty

> See [moves README](README.md) for orientation. This is the eighth design move.

Users own their identity, portable between instances. Pseudonymity is permitted; one-human-one-account verification (proof-of-personhood) at registration *without revealing* the underlying identity.

- *Why:* Lord-controlled identity is a vector for vassalage; the alternative needs to make exit cheap.
- *Cost:* Proof-of-personhood schemes are immature; sybil resistance without identity disclosure is technically hard.
