# Status-Blind Defaults

> See [moves README](README.md) for orientation. This is the second design move.

No public follower counts, no like/repost counters, no verified badges. Reputation is contextual to the topic and is not a portable score.

- *Why:* Disregard of status is constitutive of the [bourgeois public sphere](../../bourgeois-public-sphere.md). Visible status amplifies it.
- *Cost:* Discovery is harder. "Reputable contributor" signals are weaker. Trolls cannot be filtered by social proof.
