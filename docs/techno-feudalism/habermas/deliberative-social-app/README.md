# A Deliberative Social-Media App — Design Sketch

> See the [Habermas notes](../README.md) for orientation. This folder is a thought experiment: what would a social platform look like whose architecture is *congruent with* Habermas's normative framework rather than perpendicular to it? It applies the diagnostic — especially [refeudalization-1962](../refeudalization-1962.md), [communicative-action](../communicative-action.md), and the [critiques](../critiques.md) on plural counterpublics — to concrete product decisions.

The point is not to design the perfect app but to use the framework as a directional compass. Each design move is derived from a Habermasian principle and each comes with a known tension or failure mode that the sketch acknowledges rather than hides.

## Files in this folder

- [rejections.md](rejections.md) — what mainstream platform features Habermas's framework rules out by construction. The negative list is most of the work.
- [moves/](moves/) — eight first-principles design moves, each in its own file: topic-centred organisation, status-blind defaults, communicative-action affordances, federation for plural counterpublics, non-lord gatekeeping, reader-funded economics, algorithmic transparency, identity sovereignty.
- [tensions.md](tensions.md) — three structural tensions no design move solves.
- [partial-existing-systems.md](partial-existing-systems.md) — what already approximates the sketch (Mastodon, Lobste.rs, Substack, Kialo, Discourse) and what's missing.
- [open-questions.md](open-questions.md) — four open design questions including the sharpest one (is "Habermas-inspired social media" a category mistake?).
- [de-otio-fit.md](de-otio-fit.md) — three buildable slices for De Otio and the recommendation that the framework is more useful as an evaluator than as a product spec.
