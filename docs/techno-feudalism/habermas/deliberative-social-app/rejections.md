# What Habermas Would Reject by Construction

> See the [folder README](README.md) for orientation. This file lists the structural features of mainstream platforms that a Habermas-inspired alternative cannot have.

Working backward from the [refeudalization](../refeudalization-1962.md) diagnosis and the [ideal speech situation](../communicative-action.md), the following are ruled out by construction:

- **Engagement-optimised algorithmic feeds.** They favour strategic action over communicative action, manufacture inflammation, and reduce participants to audience.
- **Advertising as primary revenue.** Makes user attention the product; creates a structural interest in fragmentation, addiction, and surveillance.
- **Status display as a primary affordance.** Follower counts, verified badges, like counts, viral metrics — these are forms of representational publicness, not opinion-formation.
- **Lord-controlled identity and moderation.** Unilateral terms, opaque enforcement, no appeal. The opposite of Lockean consent and Habermasian non-coercion.
- **Personalisation that breaks shared exposure.** A public sphere requires roughly common reference; algorithmic individuation destroys that condition.
- **Behavioural surveillance.** The user-as-feedstock arrangement is incompatible with the Kantian end-in-themselves principle the framework inherits.

That negative list is most of the work. The positive design — the eight [moves/](moves/) — follows from it.
