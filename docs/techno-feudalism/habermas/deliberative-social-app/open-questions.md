# Open Design Questions

> See [folder README](README.md) for orientation.

- How does discovery work without either algorithmic personalisation or status-display proxies for quality? "Curator picks" pushes the question one level up: how are curators selected, and how is curator quality measured without the things we just removed?
- Can the platform have *any* synchronous feedback (like-equivalents, immediate-response indicators) without sliding into engagement-driven dynamics? The slow-feedback purist version may be too austere even for the audience that wants this.
- What is the failure mode of [communicative-action affordances](moves/communicative-action-affordances.md)? In practice, structured-argument tools attract a particular demographic and exclude others. Does the deliberation infrastructure itself produce a counterpublic that excludes the people Fraser was concerned about?
- The honest hard one: is *a Habermas-inspired social media app* a category mistake? Habermas's framework is about the conditions for *opinion-formation*, not for all human social interaction. Most of what people do on social platforms — share photos, joke, flirt, organise small-group plans, perform identity — is not opinion-formation in his sense and is not improved by being treated as if it were. The honest move may be to *not* build a social platform but a *deliberative-publication-and-discussion* platform, smaller in scope, that explicitly disclaims being the place for all online sociality. Less ambitious. More honest.
