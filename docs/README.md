## A note on origin

This research originated in De Otio's internal notes repository, where the techno-feudalism critique was developed alongside the broader De Otio strategy work. It was extracted into Mündig when the design implications became concrete enough to warrant a project of their own. The two repositories may diverge over time — the canonical Mündig research lives here.
